About ErasureX

Asset Trust Platform for secure data sanitization, verification and responsible reuse.

What is ErasureX?

ErasureX is a secure data sanitization platform designed to securely erase data from storage devices and provide verifiable proof that the data sanitization process has been completed successfully.

The platform follows a Wipe → Verify → Prove → Track → Reuse approach, helping organizations build trust in the lifecycle of retired and reusable digital assets.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make secure data sanitization transparent, verifiable and easy to audit while supporting the safe reuse and responsible disposal of digital assets.

Key Features

Secure Wiping

Supports a structured sanitization workflow for storage devices.

Verification

Validates the sanitization result before issuing proof.

Digital Certificate

Generates a certificate containing device, method and verification information.

Audit Trail

Maintains records of processed assets for tracking and auditing.

How ErasureX Works

WIPE → VERIFY → PROVE → TRACK → REUSE

Each processed asset can be associated with a unique certificate and verification information, allowing organizations to demonstrate that sanitization was successfully performed.

Standards & Security

ErasureX is designed around secure media sanitization principles and references NIST SP 800-88 as a guiding standard for sanitization workflows.
